## Redesigned to look the part

- **A new case-file look throughout.** Quick Coach, the Full Report, and the detail header have all been rebuilt to the dossier aesthetic, with squared-off buttons, a consistent type ramp in Settings, and cleaner share images.
- **One contact, drawn the same everywhere.** A person now looks the same on every screen they appear on, with their name and photo settled before anything is labeled.
- **Freshness in a single statement.** The detail header now says clearly how current a Dossier is.

## Clearer sign-in and permissions

- **See the cost before you commit.** The paywall now lays out its questions plainly, and the age and relationship questions are asked up front. You answer the relationship question before your first run, not after spending a credit.
- **Know what a re-run costs.** Re-running a Dossier now shows its cost before you confirm, in a branded sheet rather than a plain alert.
- **Full Disk Access, handled honestly.** The permission screen clears faster and no longer asks for an unnecessary relaunch. If Full Disk Access is revoked mid-session, the app tells you instead of quietly failing.
- **Straight talk about your data.** Settings no longer claims "Connected" when reads are failing. When your texts can't be read, the app says so wherever it would otherwise imply otherwise.

## Your Dossiers, kept

- **Returning users can read what they paid for.** Purchased Dossiers stay readable, and a synced Dossier keeps a usable name even without local message data (a phone number, when that's all there is).
- **No merged people, threads, or accounts.** Two people, two conversations, or two accounts are never combined, and empty-library and first-run states each get their own clear recovery action.
- **What's New, built in.** A finished What's New view surfaces changes in the app.

## Privacy and correctness

- **Private data stays masked and correctly attributed.** Several paths where information could appear unmasked or tied to the wrong person are closed, and no data from a previous account is served during a sign-in swap.
- **Sign-in tokens are harder to intercept.** The sign-in token no longer rides a URL scheme another app could claim, and checkout links are required to be https before they open.
- **Honest analytics disclosure.** The app now states plainly that it derives an approximate city, not a precise location.

## Wording

- **"Run," not "generate."** Small changes throughout so the app says what it actually does, including what the website counts before it starts counting and what a sealed label protects (the handle, not your identity).
